Unground Ball Bearings.

This bearing is used in a wide range of applications, such as: by grain auger and farm equipment manufacturers in wheels, conveyers, lawn mowers, idler pulleys, etc.  It has been used, very successfully at speeds up to and exceeding 2500 RPM in such uses as V belt tightener pulleys.  They are made primarily for straight radial service, however, due to deep ball grooves they are being used for thrust loads as well. This bearing is constructed with more accuracy, heavier heat treatment and held to closer tolerances than the average unground ball bearing.  The ball race ways are machine formed to a smooth finish, the outer races stamped and coined from cold rolled steel.  These are then heat treated to a uniform hardness for long wearing ability.  They contain a full compliment of balls and are furnished either shielded and grease packed or shielded and grease packed with neoprene seals.
