# Don Dye Company: art direction

**Read:** a Kingman, Kansas maker of pressed-steel idler pulleys, drive pulleys and bearings. The logo is a green oval with a rounded italic lowercase "don dye". It should feel like a practical Plains-industrial catalog: friendly, sturdy, highly legible for part-number lookup.

- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (display, 700/800, h1/h2 and family names) with Atkinson Hyperlegible (body and all part tables; legibility for long dimension tables). Use tabular numerals.
- **Palette** (from the logo): Don Dye green `#108a3f`, deep green `#0b5a2a`, zinc-plated steel `#d9dbd6`, warm white `#fafaf7`, ink `#1a1c1a`. Green is the action color.
- **Hero:** the pedestal. The headline is centered at the top, with one primary action (Request a quote) and the catalog link centered below it. Under that, the large transparent pulley image is centered on a soft steel-colored elliptical "pedestal" (a flat CSS ellipse, no glow).
- **Nav:** classic right-aligned links with the phone, over a thick 4px green rule along the full bottom edge of the header. The current page gets a green underline. Mobile: Menu button.
- **Grid and rhythm:** 1200px max, 3-column family cards, 88px sections. The part tables are the star: sticky header row, generous 14px row padding, green part numbers.
- **Cards:** a steel-tinted panel with the transparent product centered, family name below, and a 1px rule. 8px radius (the only site with rounded cards, echoing the oval logo).
- **Signature detail:** the green oval logo is redrawn as inline SVG/CSS from the real mark, and the oval shape repeats as the hero pedestal.
