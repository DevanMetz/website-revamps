# Taylor Industries: art direction

**Read:** a medical-device supplier with a family-owned, Jefferson City, Missouri character. The look is clinical, clean and calm, like a well-made device insert or reference guide. The existing logo is a navy serif wordmark with a caduceus mark, so the display face is a serif.

- **Type:** Instrument Serif (display, regular and italic, for h1/h2 only) with Instrument Sans (UI, body, tables and specs). Use tabular numerals for part codes and dimensions.
- **Palette** (from the logo and product shots): Taylor blue `#000098` (the only strong color: links, primary buttons, rules), ink `#14182b`, clinical white `#fcfdfd`, cool tray `#eef2f6`, hairline `#dce3ea`, muted text `#5a6475`. No dark hero band.
- **Hero:** a light split. The headline sits left on white with a large serif h1 (about 72px desktop) and the two primary routes (Electrodes, Production machines) as navy solid plus quiet text link. On the right is the existing transparent electrode collage on a pale `#eef2f6` rounded tray (radius 4px, no shadow).
- **Nav:** three zones. The real logo image is at left, the links are a centered group, and the phone plus a navy "Request a quote" button sit at right. Mobile: logo, then a Call link and a Menu button.
- **Grid and rhythm:** a 12-column grid, 1240px max, 112px section spacing on desktop and 64px on mobile. Product cards have no borders: a transparent electrode on the tray tint, with code and specs below in Instrument Sans.
- **Cards:** tray-tinted image well, part code in tabular sans, and specs as a definition list.
- **Signature detail:** one thin static ECG-trace line (inline SVG, 1.5px blue stroke) runs under the hero and divides it from the catalog. It appears once only.
- **Motion:** none beyond 150ms color transitions.

## Logo

The original site header uses [TaylorInd-Logo_website2.jpg](https://www.taylor-ind.com/images/TaylorInd-Logo_website2.jpg), 325 × 51 pixels. The full graphic mark and wordmark are retained from the existing transparent conversion, trimmed and enlarged 2× with Lanczos. The small tagline was omitted because it is illegible at header size. The dominant opaque logo blue is `#000098` (with neighboring JPEG values including `#000099`); the palette uses that blue for links, buttons, focus and the ECG rule, with neutral cool surfaces and dark ink.
