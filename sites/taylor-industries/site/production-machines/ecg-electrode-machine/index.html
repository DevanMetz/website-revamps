ECG Electrode Production Machine

If your company is looking to bring production of electrodes in-house or expand your production capabilities, Taylor Industries is your premier resource.

We custom build each manufacturing system according to the specific needs of each customer, and we will work with you to find the best solutions to help you achieve your goals.

Our machines can be operated by a single operator. When coupled with our packaging system, the same operator can inspect the product, package and place finished pouches in shipping cases. Different sizes of electrodes can be produced on a single machine.
