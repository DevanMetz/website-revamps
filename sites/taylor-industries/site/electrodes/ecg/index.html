ECG Electrodes

Let Taylor Industries be your contract manufacturing resource for disposable ECG / EKG electrodes manufactured in the United States. All ECG electrodes are Latex Free, Hypoallergenic and exceed AAMI specifications. Our ECG electrodes are available in a wide variety of shapes and sizes to accommodate many different monitoring applications, and we can also work with you to create your own custom electrodes.

Features

  • Low impedance Solid Gel providing excellent performance and easy clean up
  • Silver/Silver Chloride (Ag/AgCl) coated sensors
  • Stainless Steel industry standard snaps
  • Latex Free, Hypoallergenic medical grade foam
  • Private label packaging

Options

  • Latex Free, Hypoallergenic Cloth Material available on most shapes
  • Logo Tape to help market your product
  • Custom shapes and sizes. We can work with you to develop a product unique to your company
  • Packaging configuration flexibility to help maximize costs
