# Purpose

Visitors buy ECG, TENS, tab and custom electrodes or evaluate electrode production and packaging machines.

- Electrode buyers need to identify a part and request a quote or samples.
- Machine buyers need to see the equipment, watch it operate and make an inquiry.
- Both need direct contact details.

The live ECG and TENS catalogs put filters, sorting, comparison and repeated actions between visitors and a short product list; specifications require opening Details. The electrode index repeats code search. On mobile, these controls take substantial space before the products. From home, electrodes and machines are one navigation choice away; machine pages already provide photos, video and an inquiry path. Contact provides a form, phone, email and address.

Keep the product data, anchors, reference guide, machine photos and video, and contact forms. Show product specifications directly, remove catalog controls and repeated actions, and offer one category inquiry. Keep machine and contact paths direct.
