# Garzo Products: art direction

**Read:** a lubrication-regulator and meter maker since 1939, now in Waller, Texas. The logo is black "GARZO" in a red-outlined oval. It should feel like a heritage printed catalog from a long-lived American product line: cream paper, serif, framed plates.

- **Type:** DM Serif Display (display, h1/h2 and model numbers) with DM Sans (body, specs and forms).
- **Palette** (from the logo): catalog cream `#f5f0e5`, ink black `#161514`, deep red `#a80000` (actions and links), logo red `#ff0000` (mark), warm grey `#8b857a`, plate white `#fffdf8`.
- **Hero:** a framed catalog cover. A centered composition inside a double-ruled frame (1px plus 3px ink rules, inset): "Since 1939" in small caps, the serif h1 centered, actions centered, and one real product photo (for example the 501 operator) centered below as a plate.
- **Nav:** a centered masthead. The original GARZO capsule logo is centered at top. The nav links sit centered below it between two hairline rules, with the phone at right on desktop. Mobile: the logo is centered with a Menu button at right.
- **Grid and rhythm:** 1120px max, 3-column product "plates" with figure numbers, 96px sections.
- **Cards:** catalog plates, a plate-white panel with a thin ink border. The product image is transparent where possible and the model number is set in serif ("No. 501").
- **Signature detail:** the original capsule logo plus catalog figure numbering on plates.

## Logo

The original site displays a graphic logo at https://garzoproducts.com/images/225logo.gif (225 × 68 pixels). The unmodified GIF is saved in `img-originals/logo/original.gif`. Its blue exterior matched the original header; it was made transparent, preserving the white capsule, red outline and black letters, then enlarged to 900 × 272 with Lanczos for the header and footer. A small version of the same mark is used for the favicon. The source is low resolution.

Dominant source colors are black `#000000`, red `#ff0000`, white `#ffffff`, and exterior blue `#0c2083`. The cream and ink retain the catalog direction; deep red `#a80000` is a darker shade of the logo red for accessible actions and text.
