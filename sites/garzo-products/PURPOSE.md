# Purpose

Visitors maintain or specify lubrication equipment for stationary machinery and natural gas compression.

1. Find a regulator, meter, control or 501 operator and check its specifications or installation information.
2. Contact Garzo about a product, or request a mailed catalog.
3. Register a product and find Garzo's phone and address.

## Observations from the live preview

- From Home, a 108 regulator is one card click away; its installation guide is another click. Contact is available from the product page, though after the detailed content.
- Products lists five photo cards, but on a 390px screen they occupy several screens. Model 213 is only linked from the bottom of the 211 page.
- The 501 model links follow the introduction and specifications, delaying access to a known model.
- Contact and both forms are accessible through the desktop navigation or the mobile menu. A filled contact form produces the email-app status; no message was sent.

## Changes

- Keep direct product cards, detailed specifications, installation instructions, contact details and both forms.
- Make mobile product cards compact, link directly to 213, and move the 501 model links to the top of its content.
- Keep the short product list free of search, filters and other controls.
