 	GARZO LIQUID LEVEL CONTROLLER MODEL 108B


 GARZO Model 108B controllers are designed to automatically maintain the oil level in engines and compressor crankcases. Maintaining the proper oil level not only prevents damage to the equipment from lack of lubrication, but also results in oil savings from loss of oil from overfilling the crankcase.

 The controller is designed to take its oil supply from low pressure gravity feed tanks to high pressure headers. In the 108B system, the standard valve assembly is engineered to work in conjunction with atmospheric tanks or up to 15 psig oil supply pressures. This feature will allow the controller to operate with adequate oil supply pressures. This feature will allow the controller to operate with adequate oil supply pressures without the problem of flooding the crankcase due to high oil supply pressures. A 70 psig valve assembly is also available. With this high pressure assembly, the oil supply pressure can be from various pressure sources, including	

engine or compressor lubricating oil, or gas blanketed remote oil storage tanks. The gauge glass is a ¼" thick tempered glass giving high impact strength and high thermal shock resistance. The glass will remain clear and will not discolor from sunlight, heat or contact with various lubricating oils.


When a level alarm is required, the controller is also available with a wide range of alarm devices. When a magneto grounding switch system is used, the controller is available with either a low level or high- low level contact. The contact is located in the controller body with the external terminal enclosed in a conduit fitting. When an explosion proof version is required, the GARZO controller can be furnished with either a low level or high-low level switch. These electrical switches are three wire, single pole, double throw, and have an Underwriters' Laboratory rating Class I, Group C & D, Division 1 and C.S.A. certification. When a low level pneumatic shutdown is required, the controller can be furnished with a pneumatic switch which vents on low level and automatically resets upon return of normal oil level. Other alarm devices are available on request.	


	Quality at every level.

The 108B system can be installed on the engine and compressor by means of a wide variety of mounting brackets and adaptors. The "PMB" or Pan Mounting Bracket is the most common. This bracket is infinitely adjustable in elevation, reversible, and offers a wide number of mounting arrangements. The controller is simply piped to the supply and engine by means of flexible hose or tubing. Mounting adaptors are also available which connect the device directly to the crankcase.

Oil level regulators control the oil level in the regulator body. The ¾" NPT outlet connection gives excellent gravity flow from the regulator body to the engine oil pan. As with all regulators, proper care must be taken to equalize the pressure in the regulator and the pressure in the crankcase.

Our exclusive patented valve and seat assembly gives longer service life and prevents the wire drawing or eroding of the valve seat. By placing the valve ball upstream of the valve seat, the pressure differential holds the ball in the seat. When the float and oil level overcomes this deferential the valve ball is popped out of the seat. When the oil level rises, the valve ball snaps back into the seat. The oil does not leak and cut through the seating surface.


The controller is equipped with an inlet oil filter to remove any sediment from the oil supply which might clog the valve assembly. The filter screen can be removed and cleaned without disturbing the operation of the equipment or removal of supply piping. If required, the cover of the controller can be removed, lifting the complete valve and alarm assembly from the controller body without draining the crankcase.

The regulator is made from 100% aluminum castings which resist galvanic corrosion and eliminate the need for painting, making the regulator suitable for offshore and gas compressor installation.

Installation kits are also available which can include hose, hose fittings, and clamps for ease of mounting. Oil supply tanks are also available in 15, 30, and 55 gallon capacities, with 36" or 56" tall stands, or with wall mounting brackets. The tanks are available with a condensate drain fitting which allows the removal of the water condensation form the bottom of the tank, and allows the oil to be supplied from above the condensate level.

 	LOW FLOW METER 300B


GARZO Low Flow Meter utilizes an industry proven gauge to provide precise consumption records for many grades of liquids. The meter is designed to measure flows from 0 to 5 gph with unparalleled accuracy.

The Model 300B uses a double action, single piston meter. The valve moves vertically, and one cycle displaces 1/50th of a gallon. Each stroke (either up or down) displaces 1/100th of a gallon and simultaneously activates the counter. The counter is immersed in oil, assuring maximum resistance to wear from vibration.

The Model 300B is equipped with a built in oil filter. The filter is designed to remove sediment from the oil stream which might clog the meter. The filter is easily accessible and does not require disconnecting the supply piping to service the filter.

The Model 300B is designed to be either surface mounted or the unit can be mounted using any of the standard GARZO mounting adaptors.

 	GARZO LUBRICATOR BOX CONTROLS MODEL 211


GARZO manufactures a wide line of oil level controllers and shutdowns for forced feed lubricators. These units can be mounted in a number of different ways. The controller assures an adequate supply of oil to the pumps and keeps the running gear submerged in oil. All of these devices are available in low pressure or high pressure configurations. All are available with a low oil level magneto or tattletale grounding switch. The low oil level grounding switch closes when there is a loss of oil level of approximately ½" from the normal level. The switch is submerged in oil suppressing the danger of arcing.


The key to the performance of the GARZO controller line lies in the patented float valve design. The valve ball is located above the seat and is forced up and out of the seat by the weight of the float on the float arm. The displacer float principle enables the GARZO oil level controllers to operate with higher supply pressures to the controller. The oil supply tanks can be elevated to a position adequate for oil to flow properly at low temperatures without flooding the lubricator, and allows the float valve to be used as a check valve during temporary abnormal high source pressures.


	The GARZO Models 211-08-1 "Little Joe" oil level controller mounts in an empty pump pocket of the lubricator. This unit is compatible with various low pressure oil level supply sources such as atmospheric tanks or low pressure header systems. The maximum allowable supply pressure is 8 psig or 182" water column. This unit is equiped with a low level grounding switch.



The GARZO Model 211-070-1 "Little Joe" is the high pressure companion to the low pressure Model 211-08-1. The allowable supply pressure to this unit is 70 psig. This unit is equiped with a low level grounding switch.	



	The GARZO Models 211A-08-1 "Little Joe" oil level controller mounts in an empty pump pocket of the lubricator. This units is compatible with various low pressure oil level supply sources such as atmospheric tanks or low pressure header systems. The maximum allowable supply pressure is 8 psig or 182" water column. This model is furnished with a built in oil filter and a low level grounding switch.



The GARZO Model 211A-070-1 "Little Joe" is the high pressure companion to the low pressure Model 211A-08-1. The allowable supply pressure to this unit is 70 psig. This model is furnished with a built in oil filter and a low level grounding switch.	



	The Model 211-PNEU is a pneumatic low level shutdown which mounts in an empty pump pocket. The switch is snap acting and vents on low level. The switch is rated up to 70 psig.



The Model 211-EX features an explosion proof low level switch with an Underwriters Laboratory Class I, Group C & D Division 1 and C.S.A. label. The switch is a three wire type with one normally closed contact. The switch is single pole double throw. The contacts are rated: 15A-125, 250, 480 VAC; ½A-125 VDC; ¼A-250 VDC; ? HP-125 VAC; ¼ HP-25 VAC.	



	The Model 211C-04-EX is a companion to the Model 211-EX. This unit is a combination unit consisting of a low pressure float valve and the same explosion proof switch as the Model 211-EX. This model is used when space is limited in the pump pocket. The Models feature an explosion proof switch with an Underwriters Laboratory Class I, Group C " D Division 1 and C.S.A. label.  	GARZO LIQUID LEVEL CONTROLLER MODEL 101B


 The regulators are designed to be compatible with various oil supply sources from atmospheric tanks to low pressure header systems. The Model 101B was specifically designed for use on smaller engines and compressors.

 The Model 101B can be equipped with an optional low oil level safety switch. The switch operates with a loss of the normal oil level of approximately one-half inch. The switch is submerged in oil, suppressing any danger of arcing. The switch can be used to ground a magneto or can be used with electrical tattletales.

 The Model 101B is equipped with a built in oil filter. The filter is designed to remove sediment from the oil stream which might clog the float valve. The filter is easily accessible and does not require disconnecting the supply piping to service the filter.	


A tempered glass oil level sight gauge is provided to allow the actual oil level to be seen on those units where an oil level sight glass is not provided. The center of the gauge glass is adjusted to the oil level elevation which is desired to be maintained.



	 A vent connection is provided to equalize the pressure in the regulator to engine crankcase pressure. Most engines today have either a negative or positive pressure in the crankcase. These engines require an equalizing connection from the engine crankcase to the vent connection. The vent line should be 3/8 " ID tubing minimum and should be located such that it is self-draining to eliminate an oil trap.

 The regulator can be completely serviced without disconnecting the regulator supply or draining the engine crankcase. The regu-lator cover can be removed, lifting the complete float valve and float from the reg-ulator body. There need be no down time for any regulator maintenance.



The regulator is made from 100% aluminum castings which resist galvanic corrosion and eliminates the need for paint-ing, making the regulator suitable for offshore and gas compressor installation.

 Installation kits are available, consisting of 6' of 5/8 " ID hose, (4) 1/2 " NPT to 5/8 " hose inserts and hose clamps. Supply tanks, stands or mounting brackets are available in 15, 30, and 55 gallon sizes, complete with 2" filler connection, vent, calibrated sight gauge, and 1/2 " NPT shut off valve. The standard tank stand is 36" high. Other heights are available. The mounting bracket can support the tank from structural members, walls or other locations where a stand is impractical.  	SPRING OPPOSED LINEAR DIAPHRAGM OPERATORS


GARZO Linear Diaphragm Operators are small diameter spring opposed rolling diaphragm operators suitable for gas compressor applications. The series 501 operators are designed specifically to be used in various industrial applications. The rolling diaphragm creates the perfect seal for friction free cylinders. It is a durable, flexible membrane shaped like a top hat with the peak of the hat resting against the end of the piston and the brim clamped between the forward and rear cylinders. Inside the cylinder this forms a long-lasting frictionless sealed cushion of air between the piston head and the cylinder wall. The fabric reinforced elastomer rolling diaphragms are produced exclusively for GARZO to our exacting standards. Standard diaphragm material design allows for operation of the cylinders in temperatures from -31°F to 248°F. Custom diaphragm material design allows for operation of the cylinders in temperatures from -121°F to 500°F.
	


The operators are designed to withstand many corrosive elements. The piston and body of the operators are made from 100% aluminum castings which resist galvanic corrosion and eliminate the need for painting. The diaphragm is a Dacron fabric, Buna-n impregnated, molded rolling diaphragm. The diaphragms are designed and manufactured to withstand repeated use in industrial applications, and the fabric prevents the elastomer from stretching in over pressure conditions. The spring has been electro zinc plated. The piston rod is a large diameter 300 series stainless steel. All other parts are either plated or stainless steel, making the operator suitable for offshore salt water, natural gas, and sour gas service.


GARZO linear diaphragm operators are designed using INSTRUMENT SOCIETY of AMERICA recognized spring rates. The GARZO operators have strokes that follow a true linear progression. A properly sized GARZO spring opposed diaphragm operator has as much force on the forward stroke as it does on the return stroke.

 	GARZO LIQUID LEVEL CONTROLLER INSTALLATION GUIDE


Basic Installation.
1. Determine the proper level to be maintained. Make sure that the adaptor or bracket has been mounted to assure proper level control.


2. The GARZO Model 108B series of self contained liquid level controllers senses the oil level within the controller body and controls this level only. It is important that the controller body be properly connected to the device to be controlled such that the two levels are equalized. The outlet device from the controller to the engine crankcase, for example, must be of an adequate size to allow for the make up oil to flow with a minimum pressure drop. The outlet connections on the controllers mounted with adaptors are connected automatically. The outlet connections on controllers mounted with brackets must be connected to an engine or compressor crankcase with no more than 2 ft of ¾" I.D. flexible hose, tubing, or adjustable piping. The fluid must flow to the crankcase by gravity. When high flow rates are involved, or when the oil is extremely viscous, consult factory for recommendations.

	

3. Connect the inlet connection to the supply source with a minimum ¾" I.D. flexible hose, tubing, or adjustable piping. In cold weather applications or in viscous fluid applications, there are appreciable pressure drops between the source of oil supply and the controller inlet. The supply pressures on the 0-15 psig model should be approximately 2 psig minimum to 15 psig maximum. On the 70 psig model, the inlet pressure should be from 10 psig minimum up to 70 psig maximum.	
	4. Most engines operate with either a negative or positive pressure in the crankcase. A flexible equalizing vent line must be run from the crankcase to the controller vent connection. Run a " I.D. minimum line from the vent connection to a point in the crankcase above the running oil level. (Figure 1) The vent line must be self draining such that oil cannot be trapped. (Figure 2) The vent line should not be vented to the atmosphere. (Figure 3) This line must also be run on those applications where the shutdown oil level is higher than the running level. Failure to do so might result in an incorrect level reading in the regulator.


5. The controller is furnished with an inlet filter screen. This screen is designed to keep dirt and trash out of the controller float valve during initial startup and during normal operation. After initial startup, the filter screen should be checked and cleaned if necessary. Under some conditions of abnormally dirty oil, or with abnormally high water contamination, a larger filter should be installed in the system. Consult the factory for a recommendation.

 	MODEL 501-18 LINEAR DIAPHRAGM OPERATOR


GARZO Linear Diaphragm Operators are small diameter spring opposed rolling diaphragm operators suitable for gas compressor applications. The series 501 operators are designed specifically to be used in various industrial applications. The rolling diaphragm creates the perfect seal for friction free cylinders. It is a durable, flexible membrane shaped like a top hat with the peak of the hat resting against the end of the piston and the brim clamped between the forward and rear cylinders. Inside the cylinder this forms a long-lasting frictionless sealed cushion of air between the piston head and the cylinder wall. The fabric reinforced elastomer rolling diaphragms are produced exclusively for GARZO to our exacting standards. Standard diaphragm material design allows for operation of the cylinders in temperatures from -31°F to 248°F. Custom diaphragm material design allows for operation of the cylinders in temperatures from -121°F to 500°F.
	Image Soon


The operators are designed to withstand many corrosive elements. The piston and body of the operators are made from 100% aluminum castings which resist galvanic corrosion and eliminate the need for painting. The diaphragm is a Dacron fabric, Buna-n impregnated, molded rolling diaphragm. The diaphragms are designed and manufactured to withstand repeated use in industrial applications, and the fabric prevents the elastomer from stretching in over pressure conditions. The spring has been electro zinc plated. The piston rod is a large diameter 300 series stainless steel. All other parts are either plated or stainless steel, making the operator suitable for offshore salt water, natural gas, and sour gas service.


GARZO linear diaphragm operators are designed using INSTRUMENT SOCIETY of AMERICA recognized spring rates. The GARZO operators have strokes that follow a true linear progression. A properly sized GARZO spring opposed diaphragm operator has as much force on the forward stroke as it does on the return stroke.
