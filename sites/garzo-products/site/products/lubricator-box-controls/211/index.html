GARZO manufactures a wide line of oil level controllers and shutdowns for forced feed lubricators. These units can be mounted in a number of different ways. The controller assures an adequate supply of oil to the pumps and keeps the running gear submerged in oil. All of these devices are available in low pressure or high pressure configurations. All are available with a low oil level magneto or tattletale grounding switch. The low oil level grounding switch closes when there is a loss of oil level of approximately ½" from the normal level. The switch is submerged in oil suppressing the danger of arcing.

Float valve

The key to the performance of the GARZO controller line lies in the patented float valve design. The valve ball is located above the seat and is forced up and out of the seat by the weight of the float on the float arm. The displacer float principle enables the GARZO oil level controllers to operate with higher supply pressures to the controller. The oil supply tanks can be elevated to a position adequate for oil to flow properly at low temperatures without flooding the lubricator, and allows the float valve to be used as a check valve during temporary abnormal high source pressures.

Model 211-08-1

The GARZO Models 211-08-1 "Little Joe" oil level controller mounts in an empty pump pocket of the lubricator. This unit is compatible with various low pressure oil level supply sources such as atmospheric tanks or low pressure header systems. The maximum allowable supply pressure is 8 psig or 182" water column. This unit is equiped with a low level grounding switch.

Model 211-070-1

The GARZO Model 211-070-1 "Little Joe" is the high pressure companion to the low pressure Model 211-08-1. The allowable supply pressure to this unit is 70 psig. This unit is equiped with a low level grounding switch.

Model 211A-08-1

The GARZO Models 211A-08-1 "Little Joe" oil level controller mounts in an empty pump pocket of the lubricator. This units is compatible with various low pressure oil level supply sources such as atmospheric tanks or low pressure header systems. The maximum allowable supply pressure is 8 psig or 182" water column. This model is furnished with a built in oil filter and a low level grounding switch.

Model 211A-070-1

The GARZO Model 211A-070-1 "Little Joe" is the high pressure companion to the low pressure Model 211A-08-1. The allowable supply pressure to this unit is 70 psig. This model is furnished with a built in oil filter and a low level grounding switch.

Model 211-PNEU

The Model 211-PNEU is a pneumatic low level shutdown which mounts in an empty pump pocket. The switch is snap acting and vents on low level. The switch is rated up to 70 psig.

Model 211-EX

The Model 211-EX features an explosion proof low level switch with an Underwriters Laboratory Class I, Group C & D Division 1 and C.S.A. label.
