GARZO Low Flow Meter utilizes an industry proven gauge to provide precise consumption records for many grades of liquids. The meter is designed to measure flows from 0 to 5 gph with unparalleled accuracy.

Features

  • Maximum Working Pressure: 50 psi
  • Accuracy: +/- 0.75%
  • Differential 1-1/4 psi @ 5 gph
  • Removable Filter

Meter operation

The Model 300B uses a double action, single piston meter. The valve moves vertically, and one cycle displaces 1/50th of a gallon. Each stroke (either up or down) displaces 1/100th of a gallon and simultaneously activates the counter. The counter is immersed in oil, assuring maximum resistance to wear from vibration.

Oil filter

The Model 300B is equipped with a built in oil filter. The filter is designed to remove sediment from the oil stream which might clog the meter. The filter is easily accessible and does not require disconnecting the supply piping to service the filter.

Mounting

The Model 300B is designed to be either surface mounted or the unit can be mounted using any of the standard GARZO mounting adaptors.

Specifications

  • Aluminum Housings
  • Buna-N Gaskets
