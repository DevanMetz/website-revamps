# Purpose

Visitors are buyers and engineers seeking custom wire products, as well as job applicants.

- Evaluate product examples, materials and design capabilities.
- Request a quote or reach the office with a drawing or question.
- Find application details and contact information.

The live home page leads with products while its quote action is visually secondary. A product example takes two clicks from home; the quote band takes another click to contact, but did not link directly to the form. On mobile the contact form follows the office and staff details, requiring several screens of scrolling. The mobile menu opens and closes with Escape. The product lists have no part-number catalog; their photos are examples of custom work.

Keep the product galleries, capability details, contact options and careers information. Make the quote the primary home action, link quote bands to the form, put the form first on mobile, and group photo cards before the two text-only product lines. Keep the lists simple.

## Images

Reviewed 30 deployed images. One white-background candidate was tested for transparency; its enclosed white areas remained conspicuous against the page background, so the original was retained. Transparent replacements: 0. The remaining assets have photographic or non-white backgrounds. Existing fixed gallery frames retain natural image dimensions and avoid upscaling.
