# Purpose

Industrial buyers and engineers visit to assess a custom heat exchanger, pressure vessel, waste heat recovery or repair supplier and contact the company about a project.

## Jobs
- Find the relevant equipment or repair capability and check design, materials and fabrication information.
- Contact the company by phone or inquiry with project details.
- Check facilities, inspection capabilities and prior clients.

## Live preview observations
- At desktop and mobile, home to heat exchangers to contact takes two clicks; the form accepts an inquiry and prepares a mailto. Phone and address are available on Contact in one click from home.
- Mobile navigation opens in one tap and exposes all top-level pages. All top-level pages were visited at both widths.
- Home placed company background before the product choices. The four short product cards had large empty areas, especially when stacked on mobile, increasing scrolling before fabrication and contact.
- Fabrication anchors on home go directly to the relevant process; the facilities survey and photo gallery remain available for evaluation.

## Changes
