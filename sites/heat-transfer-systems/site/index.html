About

Heat Transfer Systems, a minority held company, was founded in 1980 to serve the needs of the chemical and petroleum industries.

With our experienced in-house engineering staff we are able to tackle the challenging needs of our customers while using good design practices and building serviceability into our equipment. Fabrication by skilled craftsmen provides our customers with the assurance that the quality standards expected will be met and exceeded.
