Cutting & material processing

We stock many materials to minimize delays in fabrication. We typically stock carbon steel plate through 4” thick and 300 series stainless through 2.5” thick. We stock carbon steel pipe through 24” and stainless pipe through 12”.

Our material processing equipment enables us to process raw material in our own facility, providing closer control on scheduling to customer requirements.

Capabilities

  • Press braking and shearing
  • Plate rolling up to 2.75” t
  • CNC flame and plasma (regular and high definition) cutting
  • CNC drilling and machining
  • 2 - Mighty Viper B2000GXT - 83” x 77” both with HP coolant, one with extended 36” Z axis
  • 2 - Quickmill 324” x 120” both with HP coolant
  • Custom dual 50 HP spindle 540” x 144”

Deep hole drilling of 15” thick carbon steel tubesheet with 1854 - 0.758” special close fit holes. Almost 20 x Diameter.

Welding & assembly

Fabrication at Heat Transfer Systems is performed by skilled craftsmen using modern equipment and fabrication techniques. We have developed welding procedures to meet requirements and are ready to develop more to meet your specific needs.

Equipment fabricated in our shop ranges from simple carbon steel to complex exotic metals, 300 pound to 300,000 pounds units, and vessels for use at atmospheric pressure to natural gas exchangers rated for 10,000 psi and up.

Testing & inspection

Full time Quality Control inspection and a comprehensive Quality Control System governed by the ASME and National Board support the quality standards of our customers and our own.

The inspection of each unit is tracked using a Rider/QC Checklist for all customer, ASME AI, and HTS requirements.

Inspection capabilities

  • Hydrotest - 15,000 psi capacity
  • Visual and dimensional checks
  • Post machining inspection of tubesheets and baffles
  • In house NDE – PT, hardness, PMI (Niton XL3T)
  • Paint and blast inspection
  • Multiple NDE subcontractors qualified for RT, UT, PT, MT, PMI, halide leak testing, and more
