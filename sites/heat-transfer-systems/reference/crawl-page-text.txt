"Save Energy for Tomorrow"

Heat Transfer Systems, a minority held company, was founded in 1980 to serve the needs of the chemical and petroleum industries. Our focus is the custom design and fabrication of ASME Coded pressure vessels, specializing in heat transfer equipment. With our experienced in-house engineering staff we are able to tackle the challenging needs of our customers while using good design practices and building serviceability into our equipment. Fabrication by skilled craftsmen provides our customers with the assurance that the quality standards expected will be met and exceeded.

The company's area of expertise includes the following:

Shell and tube heat exchangers
Special design heat transfer equipment 
Heat recovery boilers 
WHRUs (Waste Heat Recovery Units) for gas turbines and other high temperature heat sources
Complex pressure vessels 
Repair Services 


We hope that this website helps to acquaint you with the capabilities of Heat Transfer Systems and to provide you with some of the information necessary to assist you in selecting a supplier for your heat transfer equipment.

We welcome your e-mail, call or personal visit should you desire more information. We look forward to assisting you and adding to the thousands of units we have in service around the world.

Peter Kwong
President, Heat Transfer Systems, Inc.

Fabrication: Welding and Assembly

Fabrication at Heat Transfer Systems is performed by skilled craftsmen using modern equipment and fabrication techniques. Over the past 30+ years we have developed welding procedures to meet virtually any requirements and are ready to develop more to meet your specific needs. At any given moment you will see a wide range of equipment being fabricated in our shop: from simple carbon steel to complex exotic metals, 300 pound to 300,000 pounds units, and vessels for use at atmospheric pressure to natural gas exchangers rated for 10,000 psi and up. Custom fabrication has its unique challenges but we are ready to tackle your most challenging projects.

Fabrication: Testing and Inspection

Full time Quality Control inspection and a comprehensive Quality Control System governed by the ASME and National Board assure that the stringent quality standards of our customers and our own are met and exceeded.

Our inspection capabilities include:

Hydrotest - 15,000 psi capacity
Visual and dimensional checks
Post machining inspection of tubesheets and baffles
In house NDE – PT, hardness, PMI (Niton XL3T)
Paint and blast inspection
Multiple NDE subcontractors qualified for RT, UT, PT, MT, PMI, halide leak testing, and more

The inspection of each unit is tracked using a Rider/QC Checklist for all customer, ASME AI, and HTS requirements.
