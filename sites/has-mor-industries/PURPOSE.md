# Purpose

Visitors are engineers and buyers selecting FEP or PFA encapsulated o-rings for an application.

1. Check available forms, materials, dimensions and installation details.
2. Request a quote with the size, jacket and application requirements.
3. Contact Has-Mor by phone or email.

The live preview's home quote takes one click; the form works through the visitor's email app. Product information is one click away, but the home product choices followed an introductory section. The ordering example did not offer a direct next step to the quote. On mobile, the menu adds a tap and the technical page is long, though its section links reduce scrolling. All top-level pages and the mobile menu worked in the preview; the contact details were available on Contact and in the footer.

Keep the technical tables, product types, ordering example, contact details and mailto forms. Move product choices up on the home page, add a quote link beside the ordering example, and remove the repeated heading on the quote form. Keep the short lists simple.
