Has-Mor Industries Inc.

Manufacturer of Encapsulated O-Rings

Has-Mor Industries, Inc. is a premier manufacturer of FEP and PFA encapsulated o-rings. Our precision encapsulated o-rings are manufactured to strict industry tolerances using our Documented Quality Assurance system. Has-Mor offers over 34 years of knowledge and experience dedicated to the encapsulated o-ring field with engineering and manufacturing skills to meet custom or proprietary requirements. We produce solid core, hollow core, square cross sections, encapsulated cord, as well as custom shapes and sizes to meet your needs.



After more than 34 years in business, our commitment remains to total customer care and satisfaction, addressing all inquiries with priority and dedication.
