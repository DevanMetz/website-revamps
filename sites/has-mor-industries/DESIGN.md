# Has-Mor Industries: art direction

**Read:** a Dunmore, Pennsylvania maker of FEP and PFA encapsulated o-rings: an elastomer core inside a clear fluoropolymer jacket. The visitors are engineers. It should feel modern, precise and light, built on the circle and its cross-section.

- **Type:** Red Hat Display (display, 700/800) with Red Hat Text (body, tables and forms).
- **Palette** (from the ring photography): ink `#272425`, frost `#f5f2f2`, white `#ffffff`, deep red `#a82019` (actions), link red `#951d17`, warm gray rule `#d8cccc`.
- **Hero:** a circle crop. The headline sits left on white. The real `rings.jpg` is cropped into a large circle (about 640px) that bleeds off the right edge of the viewport and is vertically centered. On mobile the circle sits above the headline, cropped at about 120vw, and bleeds both sides.
- **Nav:** links packed left. The logo wordmark is followed immediately by the links on the left; the red "Request a quote" button sits alone at far right. Mobile: there is no Menu button. The links become a single horizontally scrollable row under the logo (with scroll-snap and a faded right edge).
- **Grid and rhythm:** 1160px max, 2-column spec/diagram pairs, 96px sections.
- **Cards:** product forms (solid core, hollow core and others) as frost-tinted panels, each with a thin concentric-circle cross-section line drawing (CSS or SVG outline only) of that form.
- **Signature detail:** concentric thin rings (jacket around core) used as a section divider and as the cross-section diagrams.

## Logo

The original site uses a graphic logo in its header: [1.1.gif](https://has-mor.com/images/1.1.gif), 258 × 216 pixels. The unmodified source is retained in `img-originals/logo/original.gif`. The full mark and wordmark were trimmed, the flat gray background removed, and the result upscaled 2× with Lanczos for the header and footer. The source remains low resolution. Dominant artwork colors are wordmark red `#ff210c`, symbol red `#cc2929`, and near-black `#272425`. The interface uses a deeper red for accessible buttons and links, and warm neutral surfaces and rules echo the mark without competing with it. The favicon uses a simple ring in the logo colors.
