About Has-Mor

Has-Mor Industries, Inc. is a manufacturer of FEP and PFA encapsulated o-rings.

Our precision encapsulated o-rings are manufactured to strict industry tolerances using our Documented Quality Assurance system. Our engineering and manufacturing skills meet custom or proprietary requirements.

We produce solid core, hollow core, square cross sections, encapsulated cord, as well as custom shapes and sizes.

Has-Mor Industries, Inc. is an authorized licensee for DuPont Performance Elastomers Genuine Viton® with Viton® Tracer™ Technology.

Our commitment is to customer care and satisfaction, addressing all inquiries with priority and dedication.
