Your Questions, Answered

What is neurologically focused chiropractic care?

Neurologically focused chiropractic care looks at how well your nervous system is communicating with your body. Instead of only focusing on symptoms, we assess and support the nervous system so your body can better adapt to stress, regulate itself, and function the way it was designed to.

Do I need to be in pain to benefit from chiropractic care?

No. Many people come to Vital Tone Chiropractic not because they’re in pain, but because they want to handle stress better, feel more balanced, improve sleep, or support overall wellness. Our care is about improving nervous system function, not just addressing discomfort.

What makes Vital Tone Chiropractic different from other chiropractic offices?

We use Torque Release Technique (TRT), a gentle and specific approach that focuses directly on the nervous system. There is no twisting, cracking, or popping. Our care is modern, calm, and designed to support long-term health for individuals and families.

Is chiropractic care safe for children and infants?

Yes. Our gentle approach is well-suited for all ages, including infants and children. Adjustments are extremely light and designed to support healthy nervous system development and adaptability as children grow.

What can I expect at my first visit?

Your first visit includes a detailed health history and a nervous system–focused assessment. This may include neurological scans and postural evaluations to help us understand how your nervous system is functioning. From there, we’ll explain our findings and recommend a personalized care plan.

How long do appointments take?

Visits are intentionally efficient and typically take just a few minutes. This allows care to fit easily into your day—before work, during a lunch break, or after school drop-off—while still being impactful.

How often will I need to get adjusted?

Care plans are customized based on your nervous system, goals, and stress levels. Some people benefit from more frequent visits initially, followed by maintenance care. We’ll always explain recommendations clearly so you feel informed and empowered.

Do you accept health insurance?

Vital Tone is a cash-based practice, which allows us to keep care focused on your individual needs rather than on insurance restrictions or visit limits. This also helps keep pricing and recommendations clear and straightforward. We’re happy to provide a superbill upon request that you can submit to your insurance company for possible reimbursement.

Will I get adjusted at my first visit?

No, we do not typically adjust new patients at their first appointment. This appointment is for us to get to know you, the state of your nervous system, and your daily stressors. This allows us to provide personalized care plans and to get adjusted at your second visit.
