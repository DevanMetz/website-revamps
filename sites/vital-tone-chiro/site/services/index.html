Neurologically Focused Chiropractic Care

At Vital Tone Chiropractic, our care is centered around the nervous system—the control center of the body. When communication between the brain and body is clear and uninterrupted, the body can adapt to stress, heal more efficiently, and function at its highest potential.

Modern life is busy, overstimulating, and stressful. Over time, this stress can cause the nervous system to become stuck in fight-or-flight mode, leading to tension, imbalance, and decreased adaptability. Our goal is to help restore balance by improving how your nervous system communicates, allowing your body to move out of stress mode and into a state of healing and regulation.

Individuals & Adults

Whether you’re navigating daily stress, long workdays, physical tension, or simply want to feel more connected and resilient, neurologically focused chiropractic care supports your body’s ability to adapt and function optimally.

Pregnancy & Postpartum

Pregnancy places unique demands on the nervous system. Gentle chiropractic care can support better adaptation to these changes, helping moms feel more balanced, supported, and connected throughout pregnancy and beyond.

Infants & Children

From birth onward, the nervous system plays a critical role in development. Our gentle approach is well-suited for children, supporting their growing bodies and helping them adapt to physical, emotional, and environmental stressors.

Families

We believe chiropractic care is most powerful when it supports the whole family. Our care plans are designed to fit seamlessly into busy schedules, making it easy for families to prioritize their health together.

Neurologically Focused.

At Vital Tone Chiropractic, we use Torque Release Technique (TRT) - a gentle, highly specific chiropractic technique designed to optimize nervous system function.

Rather than relying on twisting, cracking, or popping, TRT uses a small handheld instrument called the Integrator to deliver precise, consistent adjustments. This allows us to make specific corrections while keeping the experience comfortable for patients of all ages.

Our approach is ideal for individuals and families seeking a modern, personalized approach to chiropractic care - one that supports the body's natural ability to heal.

Our Process

Nervous System Assessment

Your first visit begins with a comprehensive assessment focused on how your nervous system is functioning. This may include:

  • A detailed health history
  • Neurological scans
  • Chiropractic Postural X-rays

These insights allow us to create a care plan that is specific to your nervous system and your goals.

Gentle, Specific Adjustments

Adjustments are quick and gentle, making them easy to fit into your day, before work, on a lunch break, after school drop-off, or on your way to the gym.

Each adjustment is designed to:

  • Improve communication between brain and body
  • Reduce nervous system interference (subluxation)
  • Help the body adapt more efficiently to stress

Personalized Care Plans

  • Your nervous system findings
  • Your lifestyle and stress levels
  • Your personal health goals

What to expect at your first visit:

Your first visit is designed to help us understand what’s going on, identify areas that may be affecting your nervous system and spinal function, and determine whether chiropractic care is appropriate for you. This visit will take 45-60 minutes.

  1. 1. Consultation

    We start by listening.

    We’ll sit down with you to learn about your health history, your current concerns, and what you’re hoping to accomplish through care. Understanding your health background and getting to know your goals helps us get a clearer picture of you as a whole person and what you’d like to achieve through chiropractic care.

  2. 2. Neurological Evaluation

    Next, we take a closer look at how your body is functioning.

    We’ll perform three CLA Insight™ scans to gather information about your nervous system. These include a thermal scan, a surface EMG scan, and a heart rate variability (HRV) scan. These scans measure different aspects of your body’s nervous system activity and help us better understand patterns of stress and adaptation.

  3. 3. Digital Postural X-Rays

    When appropriate, we use digital X-rays to take a closer look at your spine.

    Chiropractic postural X-rays allow us to evaluate the structure and alignment of your spine. They provide a closer look at your spinal health and reveal details that cannot be assessed through an external examination alone.

  4. 4. Next Steps

    We put everything together.

    At your follow-up visit, we will present more information about chiropractic, our technique, and expectations for care. Our team will then sit down with you to review your case, outline our recommendations based on your individual needs and goals, and answer any questions you have. After reviewing what care will look like, you will have the opportunity to receive your first adjustment!
