# Purpose

Visitors maintain or operate cold forming, fastener and wire drawing machinery. They need to find the relevant rebuilding, upgrade or machining service, assess the work shown, and contact B.M. Rebuilders about a machine. Shop capabilities and field service support that decision.

## Observations

- Home: Services is one click from the hero; a service is a second click. Eight illustrated cards make the offerings visible without search or filtering.
- Service pages: machine photographs and original captions help identify relevant work. Contact was below the entire gallery: the tool room page reached about 9,100 pixels at 390px, forcing many scrolls before the page action.
- Contact: one navigation click opens the phone numbers and inquiry form; the form composes a mailto with the entered details. The mobile menu opens in one tap.
- Home repeated a phone band and a separate contact band immediately before a footer with the same contact details. The phone band also contradicted the earlier review's instruction to remove it.

## Decisions

- Keep the eight service pages, source captions, shop details, contact form and phone links.
- Keep Contact us at the top of each service page and add a compact phone and contact row after the gallery.
- Remove the redundant home phone and contact bands. Keep the header, footer and contact page as direct routes to the company.
- Keep the short service list simple; add no search, filters or other controls.

## Images

- 0 images made transparent. The deployed service images are photographs of machines in workshop settings, including the GIF-sourced images; removing their backgrounds would damage the scenes. Compact 4:3 frames show the original machine photographs near their native size, with full captions retained.
