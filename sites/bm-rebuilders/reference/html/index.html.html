B.M. Rebuilders, Inc. is a family owned business, established in 1981 for the purpose of rebuilding and repairing machinery used in the Cold Forming and Wire Drawing industry.

Our 20,000 square foot facility under crane has a fully equipped machine shop with horizontal boring mills up to 5" spindle, and lathes up to 30" diameter by 120" long.
