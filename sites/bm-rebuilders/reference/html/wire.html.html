Wire Drawers
& Wire Compactors

  
 

Among our specialty built machines for customers with limited space, is our 6000 model wire compactor. It consists of a hydraulic ram with 15 degree tilting table, which is easily loaded by overhead crane when in the floor upright loading position. Gates close around the coils before returning to the tilted angle and compacted ready for banding. Unloading can be performed overhead or through the front face using a fork lift.

Custom Made for all diameters
