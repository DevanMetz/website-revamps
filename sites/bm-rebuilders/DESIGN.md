# B.M. Rebuilders: art direction

**Read:** a family-owned shop established in 1981 that rebuilds cold forming, fastener and wire drawing machinery. Ninety-plus real shop and machine photos exist. It should feel like a working shop's job board: photo-dense, practical and numbered.

- **Type:** Archivo Narrow (display and nav, 600/700, uppercase for h1/h2) with Karla (body, 400/500).
- **Palette** (from the logo): shop grey `#2b2d2f`, logo red `#b3262b` (action), concrete `#ecebe7`, machine-grey `#8c8f91`, white.
- **Hero:** a photo mosaic. An asymmetric CSS grid of 5–6 real shop photos (machines mid-rebuild), with the h1 and the primary "Contact us about a machine" action in one solid shop-grey cell of the mosaic. Mobile: the headline cell first, then a 2-column mosaic of 4 photos.
- **Nav:** a fixed left rail on desktop (≥1100px), 240px wide and shop grey, with the logo at top, vertical uppercase Archivo Narrow links, and the phone and email at the bottom. Content is offset by the rail width. Below 1100px it becomes a top bar with a Menu button.
- **Grid and rhythm:** 4-column photo grid with 6px gutters, 80px sections.
- **Cards:** photos with a small numbered caption ("No. 014") in Archivo Narrow, based on the real photo sequence. No borders.
- **Signature detail:** job-sheet numbering on the photo wall, plus a thin red left border on the active rail link.
