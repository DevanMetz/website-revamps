Production & prototype work

We are fully equipped to handle even the smallest production or prototype work. Whether you require a piece weighing mere ounces or one up to 50 tons, our modern CNC equipped facility has close tolerance capabilities. Our plant is over 30,000 square feet on 5 acres.

Refurbishing & in-place machining

Refurbishing your manufacturing and machine tools should never be left to chance. Our team has the technical expertise and plant capabilities to get the job done right, on time, and on budget.

Training & mentor program

Fontana Machine believes that with proper training and guidance our employees can accomplish anything. We support vocational training in the heavy machining high school program and at the community college level. We strongly believe in these programs and the importance of industry's involvement.
