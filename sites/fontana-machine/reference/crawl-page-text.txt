Heavy machining is our specialty.

Heavy machining and fabrication for aerospace and industry is our specialty, yet we are fully equipped to handle even the smallest production or prototype work you may have.    Whether you require a piece weighing mere ounces or one up to 50 tons, this modern CNC equipped facility has the close tolerance capabilities you desire.  Our precision heavy machining and fabricating plant is over 30,000 square feet situated on 5 acres located south of Los Angeles
, in Fontana, California.  We are convenient to the Port of Los Angeles, the Ontario International Airport and rail service.

Refurbishing your manufacturing and machine tools should never be left to chance.  Our team not only has the technical expertise, they have the plant capabilities to get the job done right, on time, and on budget.


Fontana Machine has always believed that with proper training and guidance our employees can accomplish anything.  Where will our next team members come from?  We trust that our community can produce the next generation of machinists and programmers.  So we have taken the next step, by supporting vocational training in the Heavy machining high school program  and at the community college level.  Pictured on the left is a graduating class from our mentor program.  We strongly believe in these programs and the importance of industry's involvement.  Please visit the links and get involved in your own community's future.

Driving Directions

The plant is conveniently located south of Los Angeles, California in the city of Fontana at:
10703 Poplar Ave.


Port of Long Beach/LA Harbor: 
Take the 710 freeway North towards Pasadena, exit the 10 freeway East towards San Bernardino, after passing the 15 freeway, exit Citrus Avenue turn right to Slover.  At Slover turn right to Poplar.  At Poplar turn left.  The facility is one block south on the left.

Northern California:
South on I-5 to I-10 East towards San Bernardino. After passing the 15 freeway, exit Citrus Avenue turn right to Slover.  At Slover turn right to Poplar.  At Poplar turn left.  The facility is one block south on the left.

San Diego:
I-15 North to I-215 North, Riverside/San Bernardino to I-10 towards Los Angeles, exit Citrus Avenue turn left to Slover.  At Slover turn right to Poplar.  At Poplar turn left.  The facility is one block south on the left.

Nevada:
South on I-15 merge to I-10 East towards Indio/San Bernardino, exit Citrus Avenue turn right to Slover.  At Slover turn right to Poplar.  At Poplar turn left.  The facility is one block south on the left.

Arizona:
Take I-10 West towards Los Angeles, exit Citrus Avenue turn left to Slover.  At Slover turn right to Poplar.  At Poplar turn left.  The facility is one block south on the left.
