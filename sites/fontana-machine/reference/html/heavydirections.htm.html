Driving Directions

The plant is conveniently located south of Los Angeles, California in the city of Fontana at:
10703 Poplar Ave.

Port of Long Beach/LA Harbor: 
 Take the 710 freeway North towards Pasadena, exit the 10 freeway East towards San Bernardino, after passing the 15 freeway, exit Citrus Avenue turn right to Slover.  At Slover turn right to Poplar.  At Poplar turn left.  The facility is one block south on the left.

Northern California:
South on I-5 to I-10 East towards San Bernardino. After passing the 15 freeway, exit Citrus Avenue turn right to Slover.  At Slover turn right to Poplar.  At Poplar turn left.  The facility is one block south on the left.

San Diego:
I-15 North to I-215 North, Riverside/San Bernardino to I-10 towards Los Angeles, exit Citrus Avenue turn left to Slover.  At Slover turn right to Poplar.  At Poplar turn left.  The facility is one block south on the left.

Nevada:
South on I-15 merge to I-10 East towards Indio/San Bernardino, exit Citrus Avenue turn right to Slover.  At Slover turn right to Poplar.  At Poplar turn left.  The facility is one block south on the left.

Arizona:
Take I-10 West towards Los Angeles, exit Citrus Avenue turn left to Slover.  At Slover turn right to Poplar.  At Poplar turn left.  The facility is one block south on the left.
