# Fontana Machine & Engineering: art direction

**Read:** a heavy machining and fabrication shop in Fontana, California (5 acres, parts up to 50 tons) with a 24/7 emergency pager. The old header was an eagle and flag. It should feel like an American heavy-industry poster: big, loud type with restraint everywhere else.

- **Type:** Big Shoulders Display (display, 800/900, uppercase, h1/h2 and stat numbers) with Work Sans (body and UI, 400/600).
- **Palette** (from the old flag header and turbine photo): blackened steel `#151719`, flag red `#b5283a` (action and pager), flag navy `#1e2a52` (secondary panels), bone `#f4f1ea`, steel grey `#8a9096`.
- **Hero:** a poster. Enormous uppercase condensed headline running the full content width (about 140px desktop, line-height .9) on blackened steel. Directly beneath is the real heavy-turbine photo as a wide cinematic band (`object-fit: cover`, about 21:9) with the stats overlaid in a bone strip along its bottom edge.
- **Nav:** a utility strip on top, then the main bar. The emergency pager strip ("24/7 emergency pager" plus number) sits above everything in flag red with bone text, always visible, including on mobile. The main bar below is steel with a bone wordmark and Work Sans links.
- **Grid and rhythm:** 1280px max, 2-column capability features with the photo full-bleed to the viewport edge, 96px sections.
- **Cards:** none. Use large full-bleed photo and copy pairs.
- **Signature detail:** the red pager strip treated like a stamped steel plate (letter-spaced mono-ish caps in Work Sans 600, 1px inset rule).
