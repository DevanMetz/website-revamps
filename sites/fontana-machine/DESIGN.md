# Fontana Machine & Engineering: art direction

**Read:** a heavy machining and fabrication shop in Fontana, California (5 acres, parts up to 50 tons) with a 24/7 emergency pager. The old header was an eagle and flag. It should feel like an American heavy-industry poster: big, loud type with restraint everywhere else.

- **Type:** Big Shoulders Display (display, 800/900, uppercase, h1/h2 and stat numbers) with Work Sans (body and UI, 400/600).
- **Palette** (from the old flag header and turbine photo): blackened steel `#151719`, original maroon `#660000` (action and pager), original navy `#000033` (wordmark and secondary panels), bone `#f4f1ea`, steel grey `#8a9096`, and pale flag pink `#d4a7a2` for focus.
- **Hero:** a poster. Enormous uppercase condensed headline running the full content width (about 140px desktop, line-height .9) on blackened steel. Directly beneath is the real heavy-turbine photo as a wide cinematic band (`object-fit: cover`, about 21:9) with the stats overlaid in a bone strip along its bottom edge.
- **Nav:** a utility strip on top, then the main bar. The emergency pager strip ("24/7 emergency pager" plus number) sits above everything in flag red with bone text, always visible, including on mobile. The main bar below is steel with a bone wordmark and Work Sans links.
- **Grid and rhythm:** 1280px max, 2-column capability features with the photo full-bleed to the viewport edge, 96px sections.
- **Cards:** none. Use large full-bleed photo and copy pairs.
- **Signature detail:** the red pager strip treated like a stamped steel plate (letter-spaced mono-ish caps in Work Sans 600, 1px inset rule).

## Logo

The original site displays an eagle and flag graphic above the bold white Arial name “Fontana Machine & Engineering.” The graphic is [heavyheader.gif](https://www.fontanamachine.com/images/heavyheader.gif), 600 × 75 pixels; the unmodified source is stored in `img-originals/logo/original.gif`. The live page and reference crawl expose no larger source; obvious SVG, PNG and scaled GIF variants returned 404. The full image is served without trimming, recoloring or upscaling. Its transparent area is backed with `#cccccc`, matching the original header row, including the right-hand grey part. The name sits directly underneath in bold Arial on the original navy bar. The raster remains low-resolution at high-density displays.

Dominant image pixels include near-black `#07070f`, black `#000000`, dark navy `#080010`, and flag reds `#7b0c12`, `#700811`, and `#840810`. The original page uses navy `#000033` for the name bar, maroon `#660000` for navigation, white `#ffffff` for the name, and grey `#cccccc` behind the banner. Maroon now carries actions and the pager; navy carries the wordmark and secondary panels. Steel and bone remain neutral, with pale pink `#d4a7a2` for focus on dark surfaces. White on maroon is 13.42:1; white on navy is 20.04:1; bone on steel is 15.93:1; bone on navy is 17.77:1; maroon on bone is 11.90:1; pale pink on steel is 8.43:1.
