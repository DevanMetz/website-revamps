# Purpose

Visitors need a heavy machining or fabrication partner, including customers with repair work and people traveling to the Fontana facility.

1. Check whether the shop can handle the work and contact it with the part details.
2. Reach the after-hours emergency pager.
3. Find the address and driving directions.

Live preview use at 1440 and 390: Contact is one click from Home; completing and submitting the inquiry opens the email fallback. Directions is one click from Home and lists all five routes. The mobile menu opens and links to the top-level pages. Privacy is accessible in the footer. The emergency pager and business phone are available in the shared strip. On mobile, the inquiry form previously followed the emergency card and all contact details, requiring substantial scrolling.

Keep the direct contact and directions links, capability facts, emergency strip, five routes, contact details and source content. Move the inquiry ahead of contact details on mobile; retain the emergency card first. Keep the interface free of catalog controls and extra calls to action.
