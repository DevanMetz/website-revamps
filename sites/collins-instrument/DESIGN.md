# Collins Instrument: art direction

**Read:** an Angleton, Texas maker of plastic control valves, fittings, flanges, grounding rings and custom machined parts for corrosive service. The visitors are engineers. It should read like an engineering drawing: precise, gridded and monospaced where data lives.

- **Type:** IBM Plex Sans (headings and body; 600 for headings, set tight) with IBM Plex Mono (nav numbers, part numbers, spec values, table headers and drawing labels).
- **Palette** (from the logo and drawing sheets): Collins green `#008349` (logo accent), deep green `#006e3d` (actions and links), drafting ink `#1c2a33`, drawing paper `#f7f8f4`, grid line `#dfe4dc`, and a fainter `#ecefe8` for the minor grid. White panels.
- **Hero:** a title block. The page background shows a faint engineering grid (CSS background, 8px minor and 40px major lines, very low contrast). The h1 and actions sit inside a ruled frame laid out like a drawing title block, with mono-labeled cells holding real facts (company, location "Angleton, TX", phone). The real flange drawing (`flangedraw-alpha.png`) sits beside it at drawing scale.
- **Nav:** numbered mono cells, "01 Control valves", "02 …", each a ruled cell in a single row with 1px borders. The current cell is filled in ink with paper-colored text. Mobile: a Menu button that opens the same cells stacked.
- **Grid and rhythm:** a strict 12-column grid, 1200px max, 1px rules instead of cards, 80px sections.
- **Cards:** none. Products sit in ruled cells with a mono label row (part code), the image, then the name.
- **Signature detail:** the drawing grid and title-block frame, with mono dimension-style labels.

## Logo

The original site displays the full graphic mark from https://collinsinst.com/images/logo.gif (184 × 105 pixels). The downloaded GIF is preserved at `img-originals/logo/original.gif`; the header image removes the white background, trims empty margins and uses a 2× Lanczos upscale. The source is low resolution. The dominant solid green is `#008349`, with darker green shades including `#007737` and `#006d29` in the raster. The palette keeps the original green as an accent and uses the related darker `#006e3d` for actions, links and focus, alongside neutral drafting ink and paper.
