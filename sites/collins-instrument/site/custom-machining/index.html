Custom machining

Built to your requirements

Collins produces replacement parts for existing equipment from sample parts or design drawings, and can custom-design and furnish parts to customer specifications.

Capabilities include high precision machining, custom design, machining of plastics and exotic alloys, and certified TIG and MIG welding. Applications include chemical plants, medical equipment manufacturers, pulp and paper mills, and OEM replacement parts.

Materials

Hastelloy B and C, Zirconium, Nickel 200, Tantalum, Inconel 600, Titanium and Alloy 20 Cb3.
