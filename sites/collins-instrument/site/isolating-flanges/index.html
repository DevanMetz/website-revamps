Isolating flanges

  • Grounding Rings
  • Valve Parts & Trim
  • Custom Machining

Collins manufactures isolating grounding flanges equipped with a metal insert whose tip contacts the flowing medium and allows the user to ground out unwanted signals. They are usually used with lined pipe where a grounding device is required, for instance with magnetic flow meters.

Flanges are available in polypropylene or PVDF (Kynar*), in sizes from 1/4” to 36”, and in both 150# and 300# ANSI ratings. Our 1” thick flanges have a Monel insert tipped in Tantalum. Other inserts can be custom-tipped with Hastelloy B & C, Titanium, Zirconium, Monel and Platinum.

Kynar 150# ANSI flanges with Tantalum inserts are stocked up to 6”. Other sizes are available up to 36” in 2–3 weeks after receipt of order.
