Collins specializes in the manufacture, inventory and delivery of standard replacement control valve trim and accessories. Parts include stem finishes of 3–4 microinch, roller burnished. Trim and accessories are available in exotic materials as well as stainless steel for corrosive applications.

Collins can maintain a specialized inventory of custom replacement parts and offer parts with custom modifications for individual processing requirements.
