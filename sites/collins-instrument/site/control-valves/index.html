Plastic control valves

Collins plastic control valves are designed for use with corrosive media and/or corrosive atmospheres. They are suitable for chemical, petrochemical, pulp and paper, and municipal applications.

Valves are available in globe, angle, or corner configurations, with a positioner for throttling control or without a positioner for ON-OFF applications. A wide selection of trim sizes is available.

Applications include acids, bleaches, alkalis, pH control, wastewater, chlorine, chemicals and other corrosive liquids.

Valve options

The Model 1060B is furnished with a 1” 150# flanged body, PTFE bellows seal, PVDF trim and pneumatic or electric actuator. Optional features include energy dissipating trim, current-to-pneumatic transducers and limit switches.

Electric actuators and electropneumatic positioners are available for 1/2”, 3/4”, 1” or 2” split-body, globe-style plastic control valves. Collins also offers repair and calibration service for its plastic control valves.
