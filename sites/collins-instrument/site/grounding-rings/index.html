Grounding rings

Collins all-metal grounding rings fit between flanges, helping to eliminate unwanted noise and extraneous signals carried in fluids through lined pipes. They are available in 316 SS, Hastelloy B & C, Monel, Alloy 20, Titanium, Zirconium and Tantalum, in both 150# and 300# ANSI ratings.

Paddle-type rings are available in a standard thickness of 1/8”. Tantalum rings are also available in 1/16” thickness. Other styles can be specially fabricated to customer specifications.

Many exotic alloy rings are stocked in a variety of materials up to 6”. Other sizes are available up to 36” in 2–3 weeks after receipt of order.
