Services

From pattern to casting

Our services include pattern making, prototyping, machining, coring, polishing, and plating. We provide test bars and certifications for any production alloy.

Tooling services are available for your product. Grinding and polishing are readily available, and design consultation is available upon request.

Production

Short-run and prototype parts are readily handled alongside large production orders.

Local pick-up and delivery are available. Please call for more information.
