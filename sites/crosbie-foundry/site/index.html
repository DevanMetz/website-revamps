About the foundry

Crosbie Foundry has specialized in sand castings since 1956.

Working exclusively with nonferrous metals, Crosbie Foundry uses green sand to produce fine-finish castings with consistent tolerances.

Short-run and prototype parts are handled alongside large production orders. Parts typically range from a couple of ounces to about five pounds, though larger parts are also produced.
