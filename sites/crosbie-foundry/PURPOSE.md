# Purpose

Visitors are buyers, designers and manufacturers looking for a foundry to make nonferrous castings.

1. Assess whether Crosbie can make a part: materials, services, flask sizes and examples.
2. Request a quote with part details, or call the foundry.
3. Find contact details and background on the company.

The live preview puts a quote one click from home; browsing a family and reaching the quote takes three clicks. The form successfully prepares a mailto message on desktop and mobile. On mobile, the quote form followed a long contact block; the home page also placed flask sizes before examples. The repeated quote-band heading duplicated its button.

Keep the direct quote and phone links, capabilities, complete applications, galleries, alloys and flask sizes. Move home product examples ahead of flask sizes; put the quote form first on mobile and link directly to it; make the submit button describe the email handoff. Remove the duplicate quote-band heading.

## Images

The site uses 32 distinct product photographs, all with real photographic backgrounds. None were converted from white, none have source transparency, and no product, drawing or logo images remain on white backgrounds. The photographs remain unchanged in character.

## Polish pass

Product examples now follow the hero, with capabilities and flask sizes below. The mobile hero retains a compact photo; product galleries use consistent wells and limit image enlargement. Quote bands pair the phone number with the quote link. Decorative arrows were removed. No additional images were made transparent.

## Final polish

Product cards fill their 4:3 frames without enlarging photos beyond 1.25×. Product grids and galleries end with one quote button. The flask heading, table and alloys link share a compact section.
