# Crosbie Foundry: art direction

**Read:** a family-owned nonferrous sand-casting foundry in Elkhart, Indiana since 1956. Brass, bronze and aluminum. It should feel like heat, sand and cast metal: heavy slab serif, warm sand ground, molten accent.

- **Type:** Zilla Slab (display, 600/700, h1/h2) with Public Sans (body and UI).
- **Palette:** charcoal `#202124`, silver `#f1f1ef`, deep silver `#dededc`, blue `#202f82` for actions and text accents, and logo blue `#2d3bbe` for rules.
- **Hero:** editorial. On a sand ground with a very subtle grain (small inline SVG noise, at most 4% opacity), a huge left-aligned slab headline spans about 8 of 12 columns. A real casting photo (bronze/brass part) sits right and overlaps downward into the next section, breaking the fold. Actions sit under the headline.
- **Nav:** small caps, letter-spaced (0.12em), with the white text wordmark at left, navigation and phone at right on grey. Mobile: Menu button.
- **Grid and rhythm:** 1180px max, asymmetric 7/5 splits, 104px sections. The examples gallery is a tight 3-column grid with 4px gutters, like a pattern shop's photo wall.
- **Cards:** photos with captions underneath in slab small caps. No boxes.
- **Signature detail:** "Est. 1956" set as a raised cast-in mark, slab caps with a tiny highlight/shadow pair as if cast into the sand mold. Use it once, in the hero.

## Logo

The original has no standalone graphic logo: its identity is the name “Crosbie Foundry” in bold white Arial/Helvetica-style lettering with a soft dark shadow on grey. The header and footer use text, with the header matching that treatment. The original [logo.jpg](https://www.crosbiefoundry.com/logo.jpg) is a 246 × 94 pixel slice of the old header template, including a grey gradient, clipped blue corner and URL; the unmodified file is retained at `img-originals/logo/original.jpg` as a record, not displayed. Sampled template colors include grey `#8d8d8d`, `#cccccc`, white `#ffffff`, and blue `#2d3bbe` (JPEG compression varies pixels). The palette uses the blue for accents and a darker related `#202f82` for buttons and text, with neutral greys and a darker grey header for legibility. The favicon is a simple CF monogram.
