Grant Riveters will use our precision Orbital and Spin Riveters for your riveting services.

Grant offers over a 100 years of experience in the riveting, metal forming,  fastening and joining industry. We strive to be Our Customer’s first choice in riveting technologies and riveting production systems for final assembly. Our riveting machines are so versatile they offer the best controlled riveting techniques in the world, fasten to a depth, automatically adjust for product stack up height and fasten to a pressure, even collect the riveting processes data in real-time. To best understand the riveting needs in today's industry Grant Riveters offers state of the art contract riveting services. This allows us to build proto-types, research & development, and provide production parts that meet your delivery and specifications. Grant Riveters offers the most controlled riveting processes in the world today.

The Grant Manufacturing and Machine Company is the worlds oldest riveting company ready to offer you our experience at a very reasonable rate. Same day service, next day service and weekly delivery's.

Our team is ready to go to work for you! We rivet the gatling gun stainless steel grade chain, borescopes with Incanel rivets for the F-18's, space suits, seal rings and tow-missile ratchet mechanisms.

Grant Riveters is famous for its ability to setup and rivet parts in a same day turn around. Our staff is highly trained in all types of riveting applications. We rivet parts for the US space Industry, US Air Force, US Navy, Rangers, US Coast Guard, US Marines, Microswitch and the Ford Motor Company to name a few. Our riveters can be tooled for four different riveting processes all in the same machine; Orbital • Twin-Spin • Spin-Press & Impact forming technologies.
