Riveting services

We specialize as Riveting Consultants worldwide, Orbital Riveting Services and Proto-typing product development Services.

Bruce W. McNaughton, CEO of Grant Riveters has been with The Grant Manufacturing and Machine Company since 1986 and is fourth generation with the company. He has studied all process techniques and has extensive experience in all riveting methods. The Grant company is the world's oldest riveting machine manufacturer and many of today's current processes were invented or have been in use by Grant as far back as 1860.

Grant Riveters will review your product design, evaluate your current riveting requirements or existing riveting processes and sample product with a variety of techniques to determine the best process for your product specifications. We specialize in Orbital, Spin-Roller, Impact, Staking, Squeeze, Blind, Burnishing and Pressing.

Grant Riveters is committed to provide you with product riveted with our extensive experience in 48 hour from the time we receive your specifications and un-riveted product. In most cases this service is free of charge except when we are contracted for consulting services which required a more in depth study.
