Riveting kits

Drill press and milling machine kits

Riveting kits head and fasten tubular, semi-tubular and solid rivets up to approximately 1/4 inch diameter. Kits are based upon a Series I Bridgeport Mill or equivalent. Drill press adapters are made to fit the equipment.

Tooling can be transferred to a pneumatic riveter. Fixtures are available.

Key benefits

  • Orbital, Twin-Spin, Multi-Spin and Spin-Press forming
