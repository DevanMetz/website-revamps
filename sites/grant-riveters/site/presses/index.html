Hydro-pneumatic cylinders and presses

Power cylinders combine an air cylinder with a hydraulic intensifier. The stroke includes a low-pressure rapid approach and a working stroke.

AX Series cylinders have a compact shape for lower overall height. UP Series cylinders are designed for applications where an approach stroke is not required.

MOP Series presses use air-to-oil intensification and a manual approach. Each model has a 60 mm total stroke and hydro-pneumatic working strokes of 5, 10, 15 and 20 mm.

Key benefits

  • Return stroke cushions
  • Oil level indicator
  • Oil replenishment without a pump
