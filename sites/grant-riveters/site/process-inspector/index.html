Process Inspector

Force and distance control

Grant Riveters has implemented the Process Inspector™ controller in its riveter product line. The controller provides real-time force and distance data and alerts the operator to non-conforming parts.

The PLC/HMI features a color touch-screen control with a digital readout for setting force and distance triggers. Programs are generated from high and low distance and force data and saved under a preferred name.

Specifications

The Process Inspector V570 combines a PLC and HMI with a 5.7-inch QVGA 256-color touch panel. It monitors force, distance and time for presses and riveters, with configurable measurement and force ranges. It stores up to 49 recipes and has 24 inputs and 23 outputs, expandable. Sensors include strain gage, piezoelectric and potentiometric sensors.
