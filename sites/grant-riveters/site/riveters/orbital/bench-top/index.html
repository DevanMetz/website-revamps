Bench-top orbital riveters

Pneumatic Hydraulic Servo-electric

Pneumatic

Features

  • Precision micrometer stroke adjustment
  • Festo pneumatics, Siemens electrical components and SKF bearings

On average, the pneumatic bench-top riveter will run approximately 2 to 6 million cycles before a part needs replacement. Internal components are hard chromed for durability, and the riveters are designed with a 100% safety factor and bearings. The machines offer Orbital, Twin-Spin, Multi-Spin and Spin-Press forming techniques. Typical cycle time is 0.5 to 1.5 seconds for most riveting applications. The product line is rated for mild steel solid rivet diameters from 3 mm to 38 mm. The system typically can use a simple work-holding fixture. Controlled rivet forming techniques require less tonnage. We offer a standard two-year factory warranty.

The riveters have a modular design and support Orbital, Twin-Spin, Multi-Spin and Spin-Press forming. Pneumatic and hydraulic models are available with adjustable stroke and multi-process tooling.
