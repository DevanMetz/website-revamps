Hydraulic squeeze riveters

Hy-Power portable riveters

Hy-Power portable riveters work with a Hy-Power generator for hydraulic squeeze riveting. They use a heat-treated alloy steel C-frame. Custom designs include alligator, gooseneck and offset styles.

Hy-Power de-riveters remove squeezed rivets. Hydraulic stripper, spring stripper and fixed stripper punches operate at 5000 psi and can punch up to 8 holes simultaneously.

Series J, JL, JB, K and KB generators are available. Hy-Power cylinders have bore sizes from 2" to 7¼" and hydraulic service to 5000 psi push/1000 psi pull.

Key benefits

  • Push button control
  • Automatic cycle
  • Standard cylinder stroke lengths
