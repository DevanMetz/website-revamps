Orbitfeed riveters

Orbitfeed

The ORBITFEED™ system feeds each rivet from the top, with the preformed head up and unformed shank down. Riveting is completed from underneath the machine.

Typical cycle time is 1 to 2 seconds; longer rivets may require a slightly longer cycle.

Key benefits

  • Automatic rivet feed
  • Rivets fed and fastened in the same station
  • Orbital, Twin-Spin, Spin-Press and Impact forming
