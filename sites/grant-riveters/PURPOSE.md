# Purpose

Visitors are equipment buyers and engineers looking for a riveting machine, press, kit or automation, and customers seeking contract riveting or technical assistance.

1. Find the right product family and inspect its variants and specifications.
2. Request a quote for an application, or call sales.
3. Find services, literature and contact details.

## Observations and decisions

- Home lists the ten product families, but its primary action first leads to a separate riveters overview. Link directly to the home product grid; keep the overview in navigation.
- A product can be reached from home in one click, then the quote form in one click. Bench-top variants have direct anchors and scrollable specification tables; retain these and the source details.
- The quote form opens a prefilled email in the visitor's mail app after required name and email are entered; no message is sent by the site. Keep the original application questions.
- Contact details are accessible from the footer and the desktop header phone; retain both. The mobile menu exposes the quote link.
- The same quote link appeared on informational pages including history and literature. Remove those extra prompts; retain the navigation action and contextual links on product and service pages.
- No search, filters or comparison controls are needed for these short family lists.

## Images

Eight used images were converted from white backgrounds to transparency: seven product images (bench, impact, inspector, kit, power, press and servo) and the Grant logo. Their originals are retained in img-originals. No used images were transparent in the source. No product images or logos retain a white box. Five other used images retain their photographic or colored backgrounds: Orbitfeed, double-ended, automation, hydraulic and squeeze. The Orbitfeed photo's baked-in white mat and shadow are trimmed in src/build.py (original in img-originals/assets/img/). Product images remain at their native size or smaller in consistent frames.
