Consulting Services

Grant Riveters is the leading expert in the world today for riveting techniques and processes. Bruce W. McNaughton, CEO of Grant Riveters has been with The Grant Manufacturing and Machine Company since 1986 and is fourth generation with the company. He has studied all process techniques and has extensive experience in all riveting methods. The Grant company is the world's oldest riveting machine manufacturer and many of today's current processes were invented or have been in use by Grant as far back as 1860.

Grant Riveters will review your product design, evaluate your current riveting requirements or existing riveting processes and sample product with a variety of techniques to determine the best process for your product specifications. We specialize in Orbital, Spin-Roller, Impact, Staking, Squeeze, Blind, Burnishing and Pressing.

 

Proto-Typing Services

Grant Riveters is committed to provide you with product riveted with our extensive experience in 48 hour from the time we receive your specifications and un-riveted product. In most cases this service is free of charge except when we are contracted for consulting services which required a more in depth study.

 

Contract Riveting Services

Grant Riveters since 1986 has provided riveting services to a variety of customer's locally and abroad. We specialize in all types of riveting in the most basic applications to most difficult. Many of our customers are government contracts or customer's who have government contracts. A few of the products we have supplied riveting services for are Gatling Gun Chain assemblies made from aircraft grade stainless steel, Borescopes for F-18's, electrical switch assemblies for Microswitch, Space suit flanges, Tow Missile Armature assemblies and many Inconel, Monel and Titanium rivet materials in a variety of part assemblies for other government applications. We do service all types of customers though. Send us your requirements and we will provide you with certified results.
