# Grant Riveters: art direction

**Read:** a Michigan machine builder of riveting machines (the Grant Kingsbury line dates to 1905). The logo is a heavy red slanted "Grant" with dark "Riveters". It should feel like a confident machine-tool catalog, shot as a product line-up.

- **Type:** Barlow Semi Condensed (display, 700/800 *italic*, echoing the slanted logo, used for h1/h2 and model names) with Barlow (body and tables, 400/600).
- **Palette** (from the logo): Grant red `#c62026`, graphite `#26292c`, aluminum `#e7e8e9`, paper `#f7f7f6`, rule `#cfd2d4`. Red is reserved for the primary action and the active tab.
- **Hero:** the line-up. The headline and actions sit top-left on a light aluminum field. Below them, the real transparent machine cut-outs (bench, impact, power, press, servo, inspector and kit, as available) stand side by side on a single 2px graphite baseline "floor", bottoms aligned, each labeled with its real family name. On mobile it becomes a horizontal scroll-snap row.
- **Nav:** two rows. Row 1 is the masthead: logo wordmark at left, then the phone and the "Request a quote" red button at right. Row 2 is a tab row of product families and pages, with a ruled top and the active tab underlined in red. Mobile: masthead plus a Menu button.
- **Grid and rhythm:** 1280px max, a 4-column product grid, 96px sections. Spec tables use a condensed face with tabular numerals and zebra rows in `#f1f2f2`.
- **Cards:** machine image on aluminum with the model name in italic condensed. Square corners, no shadows.
- **Signature detail:** the baseline floor line with machines standing on it, reused as the card image baseline across product pages.
